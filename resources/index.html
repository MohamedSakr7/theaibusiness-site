Good to know

A few quick answers.

What is The AI Business resource library?

A free collection of B2B go-to-market guides, AI workflows and an interactive assessment. Use it to find a growth bottleneck, plan your go-to-market, prepare sales calls, check your visibility in AI answers or choose your GTM tools.

Who are these resources for?

B2B founders and marketing and sales teams, especially at service and software companies. Each resource focuses on a specific task, so you can start with the problem you need to solve.

How do I get access?

Enter your full name and email once. The library opens immediately on this page, with direct links to the guides and assessment. There is no payment and no download email to wait for. Submitting also subscribes you to The AI Business newsletter; you can unsubscribe at any time.

Do I need a paid AI account?

The resources are free. Some workflows use tools such as ChatGPT or Claude, whose features and account requirements vary. Each guide explains which tools it uses.

Will I need to sign up again?

This website remembers your access in the browser you use. If you change device or browser, or clear your browser storage, enter your details again to reopen the library.
