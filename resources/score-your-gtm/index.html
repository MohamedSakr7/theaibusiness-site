The overview

Where is the leak?

Where is your go-to-market leaking? This free assessment asks twelve questions about how you find customers and turn them into revenue. It takes about three minutes and gives you a score out of 100, the one thing capping your growth, and what to do about it.

It looks at six areas: your offer, getting found, turning interest into conversations, closing, tracking, and whether your AI tools return anything. Use the result to decide where to focus before adding more activity or another tool.

A few questions

Before you start.

What is Score your go-to-market?

It is a free, twelve-question self-assessment for B2B companies. You get a score out of 100, a breakdown across six areas, and a bottleneck to focus on next.

How long does it take?

Allow about three minutes to answer the assessment questions.

Who is it for?

It is built for B2B companies selling services or software, whether they use inbound or outbound to find customers.

Is this a full go-to-market audit?

No. The result is based on your answers. A full audit examines what your marketing and sales actually do, using evidence and real business numbers.
