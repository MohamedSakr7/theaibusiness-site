Before you start

The twelve assessment questions

These are the scored questions, exactly as they appear in the tool. Scoring, results and next steps are inside the unlocked assessment.

  1. If you asked five recent customers why they chose you, would they say roughly the same thing?
  2. How clearly can you describe who you're for?
  3. Each month, do new people who've never heard of you find out about you?
  4. If you stopped all marketing tomorrow, how long until new enquiries dried up?
  5. Of the people who show interest, how many end up in a real conversation with you?
  6. How quickly does someone hear back from a real person?
  7. Do you know what share of your sales conversations turn into deals?
  8. When you lose a deal, do you know why?
  9. Could you show me, today, what happened to every enquiry from the last three months?
  10. Is one person responsible for these numbers?
  11. Of the AI tools you've added in the last year, how many are still genuinely used?
  12. Before adding a tool, do you decide what it has to improve?
A few questions

Before you start.

What does the assessment score?

Your offer, getting found, turning interest into conversations, closing, tracking and AI tools. There are twelve scored questions, plus two short questions about your business context.

How long does it take?

Allow about three minutes. The tool runs on this page after you unlock it.

Is this a full go-to-market audit?

No. This is a self-assessment based on your answers. It does not inspect your CRM, verify your numbers or test your sales process.

Can I read the questions before signing up?

Yes. All twelve scored questions are public on this page. Scoring, your result and the suggested next steps appear inside the unlocked assessment.
