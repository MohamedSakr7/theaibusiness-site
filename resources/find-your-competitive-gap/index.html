The overview

About this guide

Use one setup prompt and six guided steps in Claude or ChatGPT to compare your business with three competitors. Build a claims-vs-proof map, choose a position you can support, and draft the headline, proof points and sales answer that explain why a buyer should choose you. The guide includes a fictional worked example, blank worksheets and a 14-day rollout plan.

Inside the guide

What's inside

  1. One setup and six guided steps

    Interview your business, collect competitor claims, check proof, read buyer feedback, pick your edge and write the words.

  2. A claims-vs-proof map

    Compare four companies across eight themes and mark claims Proven, Weak or None. Find evidence you have but do not show.

  3. Buyer feedback and an edge scorecard

    Use review quotes and won/lost deal notes, then score possible positions on buyer interest, proof, difference and consistency.

  4. A worked example and blank worksheets

    Follow fictional company Fellgate IT through the map, position scores and before-and-after homepage copy. Use the blank map and scorecard yourself.

  5. Copy and a plan to use it

    Draft a headline, subheadline, three proof points, a sales answer and a one-line pitch, with a 30-day proof list and 14-day rollout.

Who it's for

Who it's for

  • Founders and leaders of B2B service and software companies, roughly $500k to $15M in revenue.
  • Teams hearing that they look similar to competitors or losing compared deals on price.
  • People who can bring website pages, customer reviews, deal notes and evidence of their work.
Put it to work

What you'll be able to do

  • See where you and your competitors make the same claims, and who can support them.
  • Choose a position buyers care about that you can prove and keep delivering.
  • Draft consistent messaging for your homepage, proposals and sales calls.
  • Identify the proof you still need before making a claim public.
A few questions

Before you start.

What does “a gap you can own” mean?

A position that buyers value, that you can prove today, that your three competitors would struggle to claim truthfully, and that you can keep delivering. The guide scores candidate positions against all four tests.

What do I need to run the workflow?

Claude or ChatGPT with web search, your website and three relevant competitors, customer reviews and your own proof and deal notes. If a page cannot be opened, paste its text. The guide includes a collection checklist.

How long does it take?

The guide estimates about two hours for a full run, mostly gathering pages and reviews. It also gives a shorter route through the interview, claims, proof and position steps. Allow extra time to collect missing evidence.

Is the worked example a real client result?

No. Fellgate IT and its competitors are invented companies. Their pages, reviews, deal notes and figures are fictional inputs used to demonstrate the workflow, not client results or a promise of performance.
