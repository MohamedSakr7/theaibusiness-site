The overview

About this guide

You run each question three times in each tool, record whether you are named and who appears instead, then score each tool separately. Three ChatGPT project prompts help prepare the questions and turn the evidence into a fix plan. The result is a snapshot to compare over time, not a guarantee that AI will recommend you.

Inside the guide

What's inside

  1. Twelve buying questions

    Problem, shortlist, comparison and recommendation questions, with placeholders and examples.

  2. A fair test setup

    Temporary chats, personalisation checks and repeat runs to reduce the effect of your own chat history.

  3. A results tracker

    Record whether you are named, your position, competitors and whether the description is right.

  4. A score for each tool

    Score ChatGPT and Gemini separately out of 36, then check which question group is weakest.

  5. Three project prompts and a fix plan

    Prepare your questions, bring the results back to the project and decide what to review before the next run.

Who it's for

  • Teams that want a repeatable AI visibility check rather than one reassuring screenshot.
Put it to work

What you'll be able to do

  • Write relevant questions in your buyers' words.
  • Record where your company appears and who gets recommended instead.
  • Identify a weak question group and the sources or content to investigate.
  • Keep a baseline for a later repeat of the same test.
A few questions

Before you start.

Which AI tools does the guide test?

The tracker covers ChatGPT and Gemini, with three runs of each of the twelve questions in each tool. The project prompts are written for ChatGPT.

Why repeat every question three times?

AI answers vary between runs. Repeating the questions gives you a more useful snapshot than relying on one answer. Keep the tools' results separate.

Will this make ChatGPT recommend us?

It helps you measure your current visibility and choose what to investigate or fix. It does not guarantee recommendations, citations or a particular future score.
