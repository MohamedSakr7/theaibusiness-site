The overview

About this guide

Work through an intake and six guided steps in one AI chat: score the offer, read the buyer evidence, compare three competitors and the option of doing nothing, choose upgrades, write the new offer and plan a test on real deals. Run the included Claude skill or use the project setup and prompts in Claude or ChatGPT. Allow about 70 minutes, plus time to gather your material; each step waits for your input.

The guide includes a six-lever scorecard, 24 upgrade ideas, a complete worked example and prompts for offer, homepage, pitch and proposal copy. Kitewell, its competitors, reviews and deal figures are invented to demonstrate the workflow. Its improved score is an estimate, not a measured sales result. You choose the changes, supply prices, review the drafts and test the offer with buyers.

Inside the guide

What's inside

  1. A scorecard for the offer buyers see

    Score Outcome, Proof, Speed, Effort, Risk and Packaging from 1 to 5, for a total out of 30. Identify what to keep and the gaps to check against your deals.

  2. Buyer evidence and competitor comparison

    Read reviews, call notes and won or lost deals for buyer outcomes, fears and objections. Compare three competitors and doing nothing, with sources and confidence levels.

  3. 24 upgrades and a menu you choose from

    Explore clearer results, visible proof, a dated first win, less client work, a smaller first commitment and easier packaging. Weigh delivery effort, cost and tradeoffs before choosing up to five upgrades.

  4. Your offer document and sales copy

    Draft a one-page offer, scope, milestones, options, supplied prices, proof, objection answers and copy for your homepage, pitch, proposals and the person who approves the deal.

  5. A 90-day test plan and two ways to run it

    Set 30-, 60- and 90-day reviews against your current deal numbers. Use the linked Claude skill, or the project instructions and copy-and-paste prompts in the PDF.

A few questions

Before you start.

How can I improve a B2B offer that already sells?

Start with what buyers can see in your offer, proposal and website. The guide scores six levers, checks the gaps against reviews and won or lost deals, compares alternatives and helps you choose upgrades before drafting the new offer. You keep the parts buyers already value.

Does this work with Claude or ChatGPT?

The PDF provides project instructions and prompts for Claude or ChatGPT, plus a link to a Claude skill ZIP with the workflow built in. You need an account with the tools you use; check their current file-handling, web-search and skill availability. The website signup unlocks the guide and skill link, not an AI account.

What should I collect, and how long does it take?

Bring your offer or pricing page, a recent proposal, buyer reviews, notes on won and lost deals and three competitor links where available. You can start from memory, but findings will be marked low confidence. The guide allows about 70 minutes for the workflow, excluding preparation, followed by a 90-day test.

Are the example scores and deal figures real results?

No. Kitewell, its competitors, reviews and deal figures are invented. The guide demonstrates the workflow on that input; the upgraded score is an estimate. It does not establish an increase in sales. You supply your prices, review every draft and test changes on real deals; guarantee and contract drafts require legal review.
